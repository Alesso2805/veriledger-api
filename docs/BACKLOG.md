# VeriLedger API — backlog and sprint plan

Oct 20, 2026 · @Alessandro David

37 stories in 5 epics (E0 to E4). E0 records what was built on July 28 and 29, 2026, retroactively, so that the audit can check each criterion against a test; VLA-09 is the documentation of October 20. E1 to E4 come from the audit of the same day ([AUDIT.md](AUDIT.md)): every blocking or important finding is a story. VLA-32 was added the same day, after the audit's live pass found that `POST /users` saves nothing. VLA-33 and VLA-34 were added with the webapp's design E (its ADR-0004): its views need data the API does not expose yet. VLA-35 and VLA-36 come from ADR-0006: visitors get in with one click. VLA-37 was added on October 21, 2026: the OpenAPI document of VLA-30, brought forward so that the webapp's generated types (VLW-19) do not wait for Sprint 4.

**Redesign early, in order** (product owner, 2026-10-20). The webapp builds design E from its Sprint 1. So that its views never run on invented data, the four small data endpoints come forward to Sprint 2: VLA-20, VLA-33, VLA-34 and VLA-36. VLA-18 moves to Sprint 3 to make room, and deployment stays in Sprint 4. MoSCoW priority (M = Must, S = Should, C = Could) and estimate in points (1, 2, 3, 5, 8).

The webapp's stories (VLW-NN) live in `veriledger-webapp/docs/BACKLOG.md`. Both backlogs use the same sprint numbers and dates; a story that needs its counterpart says so in its criteria.

## Sprint plan

Two-week sprints, aligned with the webapp's. Each sprint closes with a demonstrable goal and its notes in `docs/sprints/sprint-N.md`.

| Sprint | Dates | Goal | Stories | Pts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Jul 28 – Oct 25, 2026 | Baseline as built, documented and audited | VLA-01 to 09 | 31 |
| 1 | Oct 26 – Nov 8 | Green from a clean clone, CI that blocks regressions, and transactions from the webapp accepted | VLA-10 to 15, VLA-32 | 19 |
| 2 | Nov 9 – 22 | No replays, atomic mining, safe peers, and every piece of data the webapp's views need | VLA-16, 17, 19, 20, 33, 34, 36 | 24 |
| 3 | Nov 23 – Dec 6 | Forged chains rejected, a reproducible image, one-command start, endpoints tested on Postgres, and the one-click demo | VLA-18, 22, 23, 25, 35, 37 | 25 |
| 4 | Dec 7 – 20 | Safe production settings, readiness and abuse limits; a deployed demo anyone can try, with runbooks and a 1.0.0 release | VLA-21, 24, 26 to 30 (+ VLA-31) | 18 (+3) |

**Order inside Sprint 1.** VLA-11 first (nothing else installs cleanly without it), then VLA-32, VLA-12, VLA-10, VLA-13, VLA-15 and VLA-14 last, so the cleanup runs against green checks.

**Order inside Sprint 2.** The data endpoints first (VLA-20, VLA-33, VLA-36, VLA-34): the webapp's tower waits for them. Then VLA-16, which the webapp's signing needs, and VLA-17 and VLA-19.

**Order inside Sprint 3** (agreed with the webapp, 2026-10-21). VLA-35 first: the webapp's VLW-34 waits for it. Then VLA-37, for VLW-19's generated types; VLA-23 and VLA-22, the Compose stack and the published image that the webapp's end-to-end tests (VLW-23) run on; VLA-25; and VLA-18 last, since no webapp story depends on it.

**Order inside Sprint 4.** VLA-21 (CORS origins from the environment) and VLA-24 (seeded credentials from the environment) first, because the webapp's VLW-23 runs its browser tests against them. VLA-27 goes with the webapp's VLW-25.

**Buffer.** Sprint 3 is now the most loaded (25 points, with VLA-37 and VLA-35's agreed contract). If a sprint does not close, move out the "Should" stories (VLA-14, VLA-26, VLA-28, VLA-29, VLA-30) before cutting quality on a "Must". Never move a data endpoint the webapp is waiting for: a late endpoint delays a view.

**Minimum ceremonies.** 30-minute planning at the start of each sprint, a 5-minute demo at the close and a 3-line retrospective in `docs/sprints/sprint-N.md`.
